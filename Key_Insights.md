# Key Business Insights

- Revenue increased from approximately **14bn in 2022 to 35bn in 2025**, showing strong growth over the analyzed period.

- EV unit sales increased from approximately **10.4K units in 2022 to 28.4K units in 2025**.

- Profit increased from approximately **1bn in 2022 to 3bn in 2025**.

- **Maharashtra** generated the highest state-level profit at approximately **2.0bn**, followed by **Karnataka (1.3bn)** and **Delhi (1.0bn)**.

- **South and West** were the leading regions by unit sales, with approximately **25.8K and 24.4K units**, respectively.

- **South** generated the highest regional profit at approximately **3.25bn**, followed by **West** at approximately **3.10bn**.

- **NMC** represented the largest share of battery-type sales value at approximately **47bn (46.86%)**, followed by **LFP (28.49%)** and **Li-ion (24.65%)**.
